About LaunchUp

LaunchUp is a dedicated program designed to support startups on their path to success. It provides essential resources and guidance to innovative entrepreneurs, with a specific focus on incubation and acceleration.

What each scale is measured against

  • Technology TRL EU Horizon Europe, ISO 16290
  • Market MRL Authored against BRLa (2021)
  • Acceptance ARL Authored against BRLa (2021)
  • Organizational ORL Authored against BRLa (2021)
  • Regulatory RRL Authored against BRLa (2021)
  • Investment IRL Authored in-house, no external standard
