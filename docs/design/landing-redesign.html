Where each rubric comes from, including the parts we wrote ourselves.

A readiness score is only worth the rubric behind it. Ours is published per scale, with its source named. One of the six has no external standard to draw on — we say so rather than implying otherwise.

Scoring is checked against the rubric before it reaches you. When a level has no supporting passage in your application, it is returned as a gap, not as a number.
